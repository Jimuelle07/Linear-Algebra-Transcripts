- Definition: a subspace W of Fⁿ is a non-empty subset closed under vector addition and scalar multiplication. Data-science view: an "information space" where results stay interpretable (doubling a temperature ≠ "twice as hot")
- Remarks: 0 ∈ W always (take c = 0), so a set without the zero vector is automatically not a subspace. W is closed under linear combinations. Smallest subspace is {0} (the trivial subspace), largest is Fⁿ
